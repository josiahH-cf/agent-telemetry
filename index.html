What this observatory measures.

Agent Telemetry is this operator’s passive, anonymized and aggregated observatory for Claude Code and Codex CLI activity across WSL and Windows. It publishes metadata rather than prompts, messages, code, paths, or private project mappings. Dollars are exact-model API-list-price equivalents where available, not subscription bills or invoices; collection targets 30 minutes, publication is daily, and live data age appears at right.
